Protadium

A personal tool, used by its owner only.

Protadium copies finished videos from the owner's own render computer to the owner's own Google Drive, so the owner can watch and keep them. It runs unattended on the owner's computer.

What it can access

Only the Google Drive scope drive.file: the files and folders Protadium itself creates. It cannot see, read or change any other file in the Drive.

Who uses it

Only the owner of this site. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
